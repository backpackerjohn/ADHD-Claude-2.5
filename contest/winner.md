# The Winner: Ember — with the panel's mandated synthesis

**Decision.** The contest crown goes to **Ember (Thesis B)**: an external project-and-thought memory for adults with ADHD, whose crown jewel is **Warm Start** — a re-entry briefing that welcomes you back to any project or thought after days or weeks away with "here's where you were, what you were thinking, and one tiny next step."

**Decision rule applied.** Unanimous 3-judge ranking (73/68/54), grounded in the adversarially verified evidence: re-entry is the only wedge stamped confirmed-and-underserved; Ember's loop is the only one architecturally aligned with the documented lapse-and-return reality of ADHD app usage; its economics are the most conservative and it fits the founder's three mandated features most natively.

## Binding synthesis (the "steal list" the final product must absorb)

All three judges independently concluded the winning product is Ember's substrate plus the best organs of its rivals. The final blueprint therefore incorporates:

**From Ignition (A):**
1. The **freeze-moment unstick interaction** ("why is this hard?" → name the feeling → shrink the stakes → pick a 2-minute physical first move) as the execution layer at the point of performance — bounded, not a chat.
2. **Sub-300ms deterministic micro-rewards** kept out of the API round-trip (delay-discounting compliance), with novelty rotation (anti-habituation).
3. **Cumulative, streak-free progress metrics** ("engine hours") — accumulation that survives absence.
4. **Free-tier unstick + breakdown** as top-of-funnel (Goblin playbook, ~$0.10/mo COGS per free user).
5. Wizard-of-Oz **operator transcripts double as prompt eval data**.

**From Anchor (C):**
6. The **capacity dial** — a low-capacity day renders as a *complete* plan, never a degraded one. Best anti-shame UI mechanic in the contest.
7. **Calendar-tethered felt-time triggers** (read-only, phase 2 of v1): "leave by 1:15 to make the 2:00," waiting-mode-sized suggestions — external, non-self-generated cues immune to ritual decay.
8. **Nightly Batch-API pre-generation** of anti-habituation notification copy.
9. **Explicit kill/pivot criteria** in every validation plan (threshold + named pivot destination).

**Ember core (retained):**
10. Zero-decision 2-second capture (text/voice), auto-filed into living **Threads**; night-safe capture mode (black, silent, routes to tomorrow).
11. **Warm Start** re-entry briefings scaled to time away, quoting the user's own words back.
12. **Year Arc**: year-scale project breakdown that shows only the next pebble and re-plans when reality changes (founder mandate 2).
13. **Shelf**: view, search, pick up, and *find* every thought and project (founder mandate 3), including "finish / shrink / retire with honor" — the AI-written closing note that turns the project graveyard into history.
14. The bounded 90-second **Doorway** morning card (one thread, one action, one capacity check) — deliberately small to limit habituation exposure.
15. **Honest billing as product**: two-tap cancel, trial-end warning, auto-pause after 45 days of inactivity ("we paused your billing — everything is safe").
16. **Import the wreckage**: one-time import from Apple Notes/Notion so past graveyards become memory, not guilt (cost-capped).

## Product name decision

The synthesized product keeps the name **Ember** — the metaphor ("keeps your projects warm; embers survive untended and re-kindle") is the thesis. Logged as build-log decision D-004.

## Surviving risks (owned, not hidden — carried to red team)

- **R1. Capture-lane gravity:** the daily verb competes with Apple Notes muscle memory. Mitigation: widget-first capture, relief-as-reward, import. Validation: D14 capture retention ≥ 40% in concierge test.
- **R2. Delayed payoff vs delay discounting:** deposit-now/benefit-later is the exact structure ADHD discounts. Mitigation: instant filing feedback + immediate unstick value (A's engine) so every session pays now.
- **R3. Misclassification kills "zero-decision":** week-one auto-filing errors erode trust. Mitigation: confidence-gated filing (uncertain items land in a visible "Ember is holding these" tray, one-tap correct; corrections are training signal).
- **R4. Organized graveyard:** memory without execution is Mem.ai's death. Mitigation: every surface terminates in one tiny next step; the unstick engine is a first-class module, not garnish.
- **R5. v1 scope:** month-one build is mobile app + widget + web app; no watch, no clipper, calendar read-only in week 4 or cut.
- **R6. Warm Start quality is the product:** if briefings are mediocre there is no fallback. Mitigation: digest-first architecture, golden-set evals from concierge transcripts, Sonnet-tier for briefings.
