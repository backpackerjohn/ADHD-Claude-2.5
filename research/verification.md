# Adversarial Verification Report

Three independent skeptic agents re-searched every load-bearing claim using different queries and sources than the original researchers, with instructions to refute. This report is the source of truth for what the thesis may and may not claim. Date: 2026-07-17.

## Verdict key
- ✅ **CONFIRMED** — verified against a primary source
- 🟡 **PLAUSIBLE / SECONDARY** — real citation but secondary, vendor, or methodology-opaque; cite with caveats
- ✏️ **CORRECTED** — real but originally mis-attributed or tier-mixed; corrected form below
- ❌ **KILLED** — could not be corroborated or was misrepresented; must not appear in the thesis without its warning label

## What this means for the thesis (binding constraints)
1. Anchor market claims on CDC + Bath + Science Advances/Pelham (precisely attributed) — not on viral percentages.
2. The product's differentiating wedge should be **re-entry/resurfacing + execution support**, not capture (saturated) or another planner (saturated).
3. Steelman the Mem/Napkin failures: passive resurfacing of *notes* didn't retain. The re-entry product must resurface *commitments and projects with next actions*, tied to daily use, not a serendipity lottery.
4. Shame-safe design and honest billing are evidence-backed differentiators.
5. Frequency/severity of re-entry pain is qualitatively convergent but unmeasured — the blueprint must include a validation plan (e.g., landing-page smoke test + user interviews) and say this explicitly.
