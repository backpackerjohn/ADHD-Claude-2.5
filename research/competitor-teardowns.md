# Competitor & Adjacent-Product Teardowns

Full per-app evidence with URLs: `research/raw/competitor-landscape.json` and `research/raw/app-review-complaints.json`. Snapshot date 2026-07-17. Prices are search-retrieved; items marked *unverified* could not be confirmed.

## The four lanes

| Lane | Players | What they sell | Where they fail ADHD users |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neurodivergent-native visual planners | Tiimo, Structured, Routinery, Llama Life | Gentle, visual timeboxing | Display time; don't execute. Reminder reliability issues (Tiimo), rigid start times (Routinery), narrow scope (Llama Life) |
| Clinical / behavioral | Inflow, Numo, Shimmer | CBT education + coaching | Premium prices ($200+/yr), reading-heavy modules, billing dark patterns; coaching unaffordable at scale |
| General power-planners | Motion, Sunsama, Amazing Marvin, Todoist | Full planning systems | Demand the executive function they promise to replace: daily rituals, heavy setup, tight-packed schedules, task graveyards |
| AI-native wave (2025-26) | Saner.AI, Goblin Tools, rivva/Nia, alfred_, Fomi, Shimmer 2.0 | Outsourced executive function | Early-stage: single-slice tools, no memory across sessions, beta reliability, mostly unproven |

## Key teardowns

### Tiimo — the category breakout ($12/mo, $54/yr; iOS/Android/web)
Apple's 2025 iPhone App of the Year ([Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net/2025/12/2025_app_store_award_winners)); $4.8M raised ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/08/28/neurodivergent-focused-app-tiimo-secures-16m-in-funding-round/)). Core loop: visual timeline → colored blocks with countdowns → gentle nudges; no-shame rollover; AI task breakdown. **Cracks:** reminder notifications reported failing ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1480220328/tiimo-visual-daily-planner/reviews)), "almost impossible" cancellation complaints ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tiimo.dk)), aggressive rating prompts. Strategically: Tiimo owns *today's schedule*; it has no project memory, no thought capture, no execution support. It shows a *plan*, not a *path into motion*.

### Inflow — the clinical consolidation story (~$22.49/mo self-serve; acquired by Cerebral)
CBT curriculum + coaching; $14M raised ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/01/11/inflow-a-platform-for-managing-adhd-through-cbt-raises-11m/)); acquired by Cerebral ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/cerebral-acquires-adhd-app-inflow-expand-mental-health-platform)). **Cracks:** loud billing complaints (charged despite cancellation, two years running — [Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/inflow-adhd-app-review/)); modules are reading-heavy, ironic for ADHD; education ≠ daily doing.

### Goblin Tools — the beloved single-purpose free tool (free web; $2.99 one-time mobile)
Magic ToDo's "spiciness" breakdown slider is the most-loved AI feature in the space; zero friction, zero account. **Cracks:** "generates a beautiful list of steps and then stops at the plan… It's a planning tool, not an execution tool" ([Thawly](https://thawly.ai/reviews/goblin-tools)); no persistence, personalization, or memory — "each session starts fresh" ([FocusHack](https://www.focushack.io/reviews/goblin-tools-adhd-review/)). Proves demand for AI breakdown; proves a free tool can win love; leaves the entire lifecycle after the list unclaimed.

### Motion — AI auto-scheduling maximalism ($19/mo+)
Auto-packs the calendar, re-optimizes continually. **Cracks:** "optimizes for fitting everything in" → oppressive schedules ([Saner.AI roundup](https://www.saner.ai/blogs/motion-reviews)); unpredictable AI-credit costs ([Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/motion-pricing)); heavy onboarding. Lesson: *more* automation of a neurotypical planning model isn't ADHD-fit; capacity-aware, forgiving planning is.

### Sunsama — the ritual planner ($16-20/mo)
Beautiful guided daily planning. **Crack that defines the category:** "the 15-20 minute planning window requires its own executive function to initiate… the ritual can become 'another thing to fail at'" ([rivva](https://blog.rivva.app/p/best-sunsama-alternatives-for-adhders), [Business Dive](https://thebusinessdive.com/sunsama-review)).

### Finch — the emotional-safety benchmark (generous free tier)
Self-care pet; motivation externalized onto caring for the bird; no streak punishment. **Cracks:** billing/trial complaints (Trustpilot ~3.4/5 — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/finchcare.com)); Android price disparity; shallow analytics. Lesson: warmth and zero guilt drive retention in this audience — but Finch is self-care, not life management.

### The AI-native wave — validation and warning
Saner.AI ($8-16/mo, "Jarvis for ADHD"), rivva/Nia (overnight auto-planning), alfred_ (email triage), Fomi (AI body doubling), Shimmer 2.0 (AI-augmented human coaching, $2.2M seed — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/10/shimmer-a-platform-for-11-personalized-adhd-coaching-raises-2-2m/)). All attack "outsource the executive function." All are single-slice, early, low-trust, mostly iOS/web-only. **The lifecycle bundle — capture → breakdown → execution → re-entry — is claimed by no one.**

## Strategic openings (what the teardowns collectively prove)

1. **Execution and re-entry are unclaimed verbs.** Every incumbent stops at display (planners) or the list (breakdown tools).
2. **Memory is unclaimed.** No product remembers where you were and briefs you back in after absence.
3. **Shame-safe design is a differentiator with proof** (Finch's retention-by-warmth; Tiimo's no-red-marks) but no one applies it to *projects and tasks at scale*.
4. **Honest billing is an open trust wedge** in a category defined by cancellation complaints.
5. **Price umbrella:** coaching $170-225/hr, Inflow ~$200/yr, Motion $228/yr. A $6.99-9.99/mo AI-native product undercuts everything with AI inside.
