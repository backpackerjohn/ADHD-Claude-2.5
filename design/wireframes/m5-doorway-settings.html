W-01 annotations · A (ideal)

1Nightly Batch pre-gen: card written ~03:00 local by the Batch job (50% off tier); ready at wake, rendered from cache <200 ms. Never a blank spinner at wake (M5 §7 WF-D1, §9). Opening the Doorway resets the notification ignore counter to 0 (M5 §5).

2Hard cap ≤3 items: warm line + ONE Pebble + ONE resurfaced Spark. The renderer caps at 3 regardless of model output; invalid refs drop their slot and the card still renders (M5 §8 output contract). ≤90 s bounded surface (brand law §9.7).

3Capacity dial (O-06) at top of card. Changing it regenerates a COMPLETE card at the new capacity and sets tomorrow’s pre-gen default (M5 §5).

4Pebble tap → W-07 do-it-now directly, whisper line embedded at the top of the focus surface (ruling D-016, flow F2). One ember primary per screen; “Not today” is always visible and consequence-free — a skipped card is a non-state, tomorrow regenerates as if today went fine (M5 §6).

5Resurfaced Spark always carries an action (look / keep — both undoable). Night-captured Sparks (22:00–06:00) are priority candidates for this slot the NEXT morning (WF-D5). The user’s words render in serif italic with the amber rule — sacred material (brand §4.3).

6Notifications (deterministic, never AI): ≤2/day hard cap, varied copy drawn from a ~2-week pre-generated pool (anti-habituation), quiet-hours guard evaluated at send time, self-silencing at 5 consecutive ignores → O-07 check-in (M5 §3, §8 “deliberately NOT AI”).

W-01 annotations · B (variants)

9Night-ASR retry: F1 failure path — transcription fails, raw audio is kept with a retry chip; night captures (22:00–06:00) route to the next morning’s card (WF-D5). Copy never blames (“the words didn’t come through”, not “error”). While retrying, the amber queued chip reassures — the only numeric-free “safe” badge allowed (brand law §9.2). Audio retention follows the keep-audio toggle in W-14.

10Phase-2 felt-time line: calendar-read trigger rendered as lead-time (“leave by 1:15”), never a countdown timer (M4, brand law §9.8). Dusty-blue rest styling — informational, not alarming; the word “urgent” never appears (brand §5.3).

11Snooze feedback: swiping an item excludes it from today’s and tomorrow’s generation; undo via toast (M5 §5). Toast is a polite live region (brand law §9.12).

W-01 annotations · C (states)

12Regeneration skeleton: only shown on dial change — previous card grayed, warm shimmer (1.8 s sweep), skeleton appears only after 300 ms; <5 s p95, deterministic fallback served at 8 s (M5 §6, §9; brand §8). Never shown at wake — pre-gen guarantees readiness.

13Offline: last generated card served from local cache with a subtle “as of last night” stamp; skip / dial / snooze queue deterministically and sync later (M5 §6). Queue chip is the single allowed reassurance badge — amber, no numerals of guilt.

14AI-degraded / pre-gen failed: the SAME 3-slot shape assembled deterministically from cached data — warmest Thread by last-touch, its stored next Pebble (from the Arc, no AI), most recent night-captured Spark. The user never sees an error at wake; one background retry improves tomorrow, today’s card never swaps out from under her (M5 §10).

15Nothing-warm: no fake card — a single capture prompt (“no data” is banned copy, brand §5.2); if Threads exist but all rest, one gentle Shelf link (M5 §6). Warm illustration placeholder, no mascots (brand §7).

W-01b annotations

1“Welcome back. Nothing is lost.” verbatim — the load-bearing promise on every welcome-back surface (brand §5.1-2). Triggered when absence ≥7 quiet days; it supersedes today’s daily card (M5 §4). No day count anywhere — “while you were away”, never “47 days” (brand §5.1-6).

2At most 2 warm Threads + one tiny step. Explicitly NO backlog wall, no accumulated list, no enumeration of what happened (M5 §6, §8 guardrails). Auto-breadcrumbs feed the “where you stopped” line.

3“Show me” (single primary) opens the Shelf’s Warm row (M2) or the top Thread’s full Briefing (M3, F3). Normal daily cards resume the next morning at her remembered dial position (WF-D3).

4Billing-pause line appears ONLY if the 45-day auto-pause fired: “We paused your billing while you were away — everything is safe.” (brand §5.2). Rendered in dusty rest styling — a kindness, not a warning. Links to W-15 paused state.

W-01c annotations

1Dial at low-spoon, tinted dusty (rest, not failure — brand §3.4-4). No “reduced mode” label, no comparison, no apology; the words “lazy” and “light mode” are banned (brand §5.2).

2A COMPLETE plan of exactly one tiny thing, framed as the whole day (“That’s the whole plan. It counts.” — canonical copy, brand §5.2). No residue of the fuller card, no grayed-out “should” list (M5 §6, WF-D2).

W-01d annotations

1Trigger: in-app only, if Ember is opened within 2 h before quiet-hours start (default 22:00). The close NEVER sends a notification and is skippable like everything else (WF-D4). Ember minutes are cumulative and streak-free.

2One capture field — files via normal F1 capture (toast O-01, Loose fallback). The screen’s only ember is the nav Catch button; the close itself has no primary because nothing is demanded.

3Silent rollover: untouched items simply feed tomorrow’s regeneration — nothing “moves to overdue”, no debt is displayed. At local midnight the card archives and tomorrow’s replaces it; “missed” is not a state that exists (M5 §4, §6 card-skipped).

O-06

Capacity dial — 3-position control (component spec)

M5 §3 Capacity Dial state, §4 lifecycle, §5 “Set capacity dial”. All three states are equal siblings — no position looks like a warning or a downgrade.

POSITION 1 · FULL (default)

Card renders 3 items: warm line · one Pebble · one Spark.

POSITION 2 · MEDIUM

2 items, with a smaller Pebble (M5 §6).

POSITION 3 · LOW-SPOON

A complete plan of exactly one tiny thing (→ W-01c). Dusty tint = rest, never failure.

O-06 annotations

1Interaction: tapping a segment regenerates today’s card as a NEW complete card at that capacity (never a filtered one) — skeleton state, <5 s p95, deterministic template fallback at 8 s with toast “Ember will polish this overnight” (M5 §4, §10). Undo by re-setting the dial.

2Persistence: position is a lasting setting created at onboarding, remembered day-to-day, and it sets tomorrow’s Batch pre-gen default. Never auto-resets, never trends, never commented on (“you’ve been low-spoon 12 days” is forbidden by contract — M5 §11).

3Mechanics are deterministic, not AI (M5 §8 “deliberately NOT AI”). Phone/web conflict: last-write-wins by timestamp, one silent regeneration, no prompt (M5 §10). Segments are ≥44 px targets; states carry text labels, never color alone (brand laws §9.3, §3.4-3).

O-07 annotations

1Trigger: a deterministic counter — a ping neither opened nor dismissed-with-intent increments ignore_count; at 5 consecutive ignores the engine self-silences and queues this ONE gentle check-in in place of the next ping. No escalation ever follows (M5 §4, WF-D3). Copy per brand §5.2; “You’ve ignored 5 notifications!” is the never-say.

2Three honest paths: go quiet (primary — the kind default) · keep knocking · edit the window inline (deep-links to W-14 notifications). Silenced clears ONLY via this check-in or settings — never automatically (M5 §4).

3Any Doorway open resets the ignore count to 0 (M5 §5). The ≤2/day cap and quiet-hours guard remain in force regardless of the answer here — scheduling is never AI (M5 §8).

4Silenced state on the card: the Doorway still generates daily and carries the quiet one-liner with a re-enable link (M5 §6 notification-silenced). Dusty rest styling — silence is rest, not punishment.

W-14 annotations

12/day cap is engineering, not preference: the master toggle only turns pings on/off; the ≤2/day cap, quiet-hours guard, and self-silencing counter are deterministic and non-configurable (M5 §8 “deliberately NOT AI”). The silenced row mirrors O-07 — settings is one of only two places silence clears (M5 §4).

2Window picker + quiet hours: the morning window comes from onboarding (W-16) and is editable here and inline from the O-07 check-in; quiet hours default 22:00–08:00, user-editable, evaluated deterministically at send time (M5 §3, §7 WF-D5). Lockscreen payloads keep Thread names off by default (M5 §11).

3Privacy & export: on-device transcription default (product brief); keep-audio governs the night-ASR-retry card’s recordings (W-01 variant); export is one tap; delete is a typed confirm (O-09) styled in ink weight — destructive ≠ red (brand §3.4-2). Dial history and silencing state never leave the account (M5 §11).

4Mobile mirror: same objects, stacked list; capacity default is the same persisted dial the Doorway card carries — one setting, two surfaces, last-write-wins on conflict (M5 §10).

W-15 annotations

1Honest trial copy, verbatim from brand §5.2: “Your trial ends Thursday. If Ember hasn’t earned a place, no hard feelings — export takes one tap.” 21-day full trial, no card up front (product brief). No “Last chance!”, no countdown.

2Plain price, both options visible: $8.99/mo · $79/yr. The one ember on this screen is “Keep Ember” — cancel/pause are quiet, equally reachable, never hidden (F10).

3“Pause or cancel” is tap 1. The auto-pause policy is stated up front because seeing it exists is why Maya doesn’t preemptively cancel — churn moment converted to trust moment (day-in-the-life, Billing).

4Tap 2 completes the cancel. Exactly two taps, no retention maze, no guilt survey, no “are you sure you want to lose everything?” (F10). Destructive confirm carries ink-weight typography, never red or alarm color (brand §3.4-2).

5Pause offered once, quietly, as a genuine alternative ($0, everything stays warm) — an option, not an obstacle. “Never mind” exits with no further prompts.

6Auto-pause at 45 idle days: one email + this in-app note, verbatim brand §5.2 copy; dusty rest styling. The welcome-back Doorway (W-01b) carries the matching one-liner (F6).

7Resume is one tap (F10) and the screen’s single primary. Paused = $0/month, data intact, nothing expires, nothing back-charged — stated in plain words (honest-billing cross-cutting system).
