# Prototyping Real Daily Use — Maya's Three Weeks with Ember

A concrete simulation of real usage — including the lapse — mapped to canonical screens (`product/navigation-map.md` IDs) and module behaviors (`product/modules/`). This is the script the clickable prototype (design/prototype/) enacts, and the test script for the screenshot verification pass.

## Week 1 — Trust formation

**Day 1, 8:12am — onboarding (W-16).** Maya downloads Ember from a Reddit thread. Three steps: she captures her first thought by voice ("I still need to deal with the Etsy shop banner"), watches it become a Thread ("Ember started a thread: *Etsy shop* — rename?"), sees a preview Doorway. No account wall until step 3; no permissions begging. Time: 90 seconds.

**Day 1, 8:20am — import (W-13, web).** Prompted gently ("Got old notes rotting somewhere? Bring the wreckage — it becomes memory, not guilt"), she uploads her Apple Notes export that evening: *"1,204 sparks joined 9 threads; 312 are resting in Loose Sparks."* Her four-year-old novel notes are suddenly… somewhere. Findable.

**Day 2–5 — capture becomes reflexive (F1).** 6–20 Catches/day via lockscreen widget (W-02): meeting stray thoughts, a gift idea for her sister (files to *People: Sam*), shower ideas via the phone on the sink. Each shows the <300ms toast (O-01) — "Caught → filed to *Etsy shop*". Two misfiles in week one: she drags one from the toast (O-02), one from Loose Sparks (W-12). Filing visibly improves — trust survives R3.

**Day 3, 11:40pm — night catch (W-02b).** Can't sleep; brain looping on taxes. Black screen, one field, no feed: "what if the 1099 from the contract gig never came." Phone down. The thought is gone from her head and safe. (Insomnia evidence: Brevik 66.8%.)

**Day 4, 8:05am — Doorway (W-01, F2).** "Three threads are warm. Your *Taxes* thread caught something at midnight — the next pebble is 'search email for 1099-NEC' (about 5 min). Also: Tuesday you had a good idea about the shop logo — want to look?" She does the 1099 search from her phone in bed. Done → soft ember-glow pulse + "12 ember minutes today." Card is finished in under 90 seconds.

**Day 6 — first Arc (W-09→W-10, F4).** She opens *Etsy shop* on the web app, taps "make this an arc." Proposal shows 5 milestones to a spring launch; she deletes one, shrinks another, accepts. The arc view shows ONE pebble: "export the banner file Anna sent — 2 min." The wall of steps stays collapsed.

## Week 2 — Real life interferes (by design)

**Day 8 — freeze moment (W-07→W-06, F5).** Doorway pebble: "email the accountant the 1099 question." She opens the focus surface, freezes. Taps *"why is this hard?"* Unstick, exchange 1: "What's the feeling — dread, fog, or 'it's too big'?" → dread. Exchange 2: "Worst realistic outcome of a wrong question? He corrects you. That's his job — you're paying him." Exchange 3: "Two-minute version: paste the 1099 into a draft, subject 'quick question', don't send." She does it. Sends it anyway. The script ends; no fourth exchange exists.

**Day 9 — low-spoon day (W-01c, O-06).** Terrible sleep. She flips the capacity dial to low. The Doorway renders a COMPLETE plan: "One tiny thing today, only if you want it: water the plants thread — 2 minutes. Everything else is keeping warm." No greyed-out list of what she "should" be doing. She does the one thing. It counts.

**Day 10–13 — mixed.** Captures continue (the anxiety-relief verb holds even when nothing else happens). One notification is ignored 3 days running; on day 13 the copy varies, then the system logs ignores toward self-silencing (M5 cap: silence at 5, one gentle check-in O-07).

## The lapse — Days 14–31 (this is the product's exam)

Work explodes. Maya stops opening Ember. **What Ember does: almost nothing — correctly.**
- Notifications self-silence after 5 ignores (no escalation, no "we miss you!" guilt drip).
- Threads quietly move warm→resting at 14 quiet days (D-005). No red. No overdue math accumulating.
- Nightly digests pause (no activity → no cost; idle user ≈ $0 COGS).
- Auto-breadcrumbs from her last sessions sit waiting: "You stopped after emailing the accountant; you were waiting on his reply."

## The return — Day 32 (crown jewel, F3/F6)

A random Tuesday she remembers the craft fair deadline. Opens Ember braced for the usual wall of shame.

**W-01b — welcome-back Doorway:** *"Welcome back. Nothing is lost. Three threads are still warm underneath: Etsy shop has a deadline that moved closer. Want the briefing?"*

**W-05 — full Warm Start on *Etsy shop*:**
> *Where you were:* You'd exported Anna's banner file and were choosing between two fonts.
> *What you were thinking:* On March 3 you captured: "the serif one feels more like a real shop but the sans matches the product photos" — and on March 5: "just pick one, nobody but me will ever notice."
> *Why you cared:* You wanted the shop live before the spring craft fair.
> *What changed:* The fair deadline is now 5 weeks out. Your accountant replied (you never read it — it's in the *Taxes* thread).
> *One tiny step:* Open the two banner drafts side by side and pick with your gut — 3 minutes, timer optional.

Four buttons. She taps **Do it now** (W-07). Picks the serif. The arc re-plans silently; tomorrow's Doorway will carry the next pebble. Total re-entry time: **4 minutes**, versus the documented "re-entry cost feels enormous / restart from zero" baseline.

**One retirement (W-08, F7).** The briefing for *Macramé* (untouched 9 months, imported) offers honesty. She taps *Retire with honor*. Closing Note draft: "You made three wall hangings people actually kept. It taught you knots, patience, and that you like making gifts more than inventory. Retired with honor, March–December." She edits one word, saves. The thread moves to the gallery — history, not shame.

**Billing (W-15).** Had the lapse hit 45 days, billing would have auto-paused with a note. It didn't — but seeing the policy exists ("if you disappear, we stop charging") is why she doesn't preemptively cancel. Churn moment converted to trust moment.

## What this walkthrough proves (and what it can't)

**Proves (by construction, against the specs):** every module earns its place in a real week; the four risky moments (freeze, low-spoon day, lapse, return) each have a designed behavior; no surface anywhere shows a streak, an overdue count, or red; the crown-jewel moment delivers in minutes what the evidence says costs projects their lives.

**Cannot prove (honesty clause):** that real users capture 6-20×/day (R1), that briefing quality lands at production model quality (R6), or re-entry pain's true frequency — these are exactly what the month-one validation plan (thesis-B §9) measures with a Wizard-of-Oz concierge before code is written.
